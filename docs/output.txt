DEMO — Budget Travel Assistant  (role: travel)
================================================================================

  [Turn 1]
  User: I want to visit Spain.

  Budget Travel Assistant: What's your budget and how many days are you planning to stay? Spain has many affordable options depending on where you go and how you travel.

  (memory now holds 1 exchanges, next request sends 3 messages)

  [Turn 2]
  User: Yes, around 800 euros.
        ^ gives the budget only — never repeats 'Spain'

  Budget Travel Assistant: With 800 euros, you could visit a budget-friendly region like Andalusia for 5-7 days. Stay in a hostel or guesthouse in Seville or Granada, eat at local markets, and use public transport — all within your budget. Would you prefer a coastal area or a city with history?

  (memory now holds 2 exchanges, next request sends 5 messages)

  [Turn 3]
  User: I'd rather avoid big cities.
        ^ adds a preference, still no destination or budget mentioned

  Budget Travel Assistant: Perfect — consider the Costa de la Luz in Andalusia, near the Atlantic coast. It’s less crowded than major cities, with affordable accommodation, fresh seafood, and scenic beaches. You can also explore nearby towns like Arcos de la Frontera or El Puerto de Santa María on a budget. Would you like tips on getting there?

  (memory now holds 3 exchanges, next request sends 7 messages)

  [Turn 4]
  User: Can you suggest something else within my budget?
        ^ says 'my budget' without the number: needs memory of 800 euros

  Budget Travel Assistant: Certainly! The Almería region is another great option — it’s quieter than the Costa del Sol, with beautiful beaches, olive groves, and the unique rock formations of Roquetas de Mar. You can stay in a budget hostel, eat at local family-run restaurants, and travel by bus or bike. It’s all within your 800 euro budget. Would you like help planning a 5-7 day itinerary?

  (memory now holds 4 exchanges, next request sends 9 messages)

  [Turn 5]
  User: How many days would that need?
        ^ says 'that' — needs memory of the suggestion just made

  Budget Travel Assistant: The Almería region can be comfortably explored in 5-7 days, allowing time to relax on the beach, visit nearby towns, and enjoy local culture without overspending. If you’re looking for a slower pace, you could even extend it to 10 days. Would you like a sample itinerary?

  (memory now holds 5 exchanges, next request sends 11 messages)

What the assistant had to remember
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Turn 4 says "within my budget" and names no number. Answering it requires
  the 800 euros from turn 2 and the "no big cities" from turn 3. Turn 5 says
  "that", which only resolves against the suggestion in turn 4.

DEMO — Strict Grammar Teacher  (role: grammar)
================================================================================

  [Turn 1]
  User: she dont like apple's

  Strict Grammar Teacher: Corrected: She doesn't like apples.  
Errors: - 'she' needs a capital S at the start of a sentence - 'dont' needs an apostrophe: doesn't - 'dont' is the wrong form after 'she': use doesn't - 'apple's' is possessive; the plural is apples.

  (memory now holds 1 exchanges, next request sends 3 messages)

  [Turn 2]
  User: me and him goes to the store yesterday
        ^ a fresh sentence with new errors

  Strict Grammar Teacher: Corrected: Me and him went to the store yesterday.  
Errors: - 'goes' is incorrect; use 'went' for past tense - 'goes' is third person singular; 'me and him' is plural, so 'went' is correct.

  (memory now holds 2 exchanges, next request sends 5 messages)

  [Turn 3]
  User: Did I make the same mistake twice?
        ^ asks about its own history: pure memory question

  Strict Grammar Teacher: Yes, you made the same mistake twice.  
Errors: - 'goes' is incorrect; use 'went' for past tense - 'goes' is third person singular; 'me and him' is plural, so 'went' is correct.

  (memory now holds 3 exchanges, next request sends 7 messages)

  [Turn 4]
  User: The cat sat on the mat.
        ^ already correct — a strict teacher must not invent a fix

  Strict Grammar Teacher: The cat sat on the mat.  
No errors.

  (memory now holds 4 exchanges, next request sends 9 messages)

================================================================================
THE FEW-SHOT CONTAMINATION BUG — MEASURED
================================================================================

  Found on llama3.2:3b while building this demo, and worth the space because it
  is subtle AND because it turned out to be model-dependent.

  The first version of the travel example opened with "I have 300 euros and a
  long weekend". Asked later to recall the budget — after the user had said 800
  euros — llama3.2:3b answered 300. The figure from the EXAMPLE was reported as
  the user's own. Nothing errored.

  Four variants, same 2-turn conversation each time, then "Remind me what my
  budget was." Counting how often the answer contains 300 instead of 800.

  Recorded on llama3.2:3b:  4/4 as chat turns, 3/4 in the system message,
                            0/4 with the figure removed, 0/4 with no examples.

Failure rate over 4 runs each
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  variant                                          said 300 (wrong)
  examples as chat turns, example says '300'                 0/4
  examples in system message, says '300'                     0/4
  examples in system message, no figures                     0/4
  no examples at all                                         0/4

  Reading THIS run (qwen3:8b):
    - With the figure present, the wrong number came back 0/8 times.
    - With no figure in the example, 0/8.
    - This model did not reproduce the leak at all. The bug is real and was
      measured on llama3.2:3b (4/4 as chat turns); a stronger model separates
      example turns from real history reliably enough that it did not appear
      once here. Model capability, not prompt structure, decided it.
    - The mitigation is kept anyway. It costs nothing, and a failure that
      depends on which model you loaded is not a failure you have fixed.

  The travel role ships the figure-free example either way. The version with
  300 euros is kept in the code as `few_shot_trap`, used only by this demo.

What the reset did and did not remove
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Gone      : every user and assistant turn — Spain, 800 euros, no big cities.
  Kept      : the system message and the few-shot examples.
  Therefore : the assistant is still a budget travel assistant, still in role,
              still asking for a budget before recommending — it has simply
              forgotten this traveller. Role is configuration; memory is state.
